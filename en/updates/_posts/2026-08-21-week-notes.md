Our week notes this week are a little shorter as this week due to members of our team being on holidays, attending funerals and taking well deserved annual leave. 
 
## Here's what our reduced team accomplished this week... 

![photo of sticky notes from Lucinda’s flood workshop](https://github.com/nrw-digital/week-notes/blob/6ebdef686ecdf2bf6d494dd4fc4160fddfe57226/images/sticky%20notes%20image.jpg?raw=true)

+ Lucinda travelled down to Cardiff this week to join a workshop as part of the Flood team meeting. She shared [user profiles, created by Gov.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/understanding-disabilities-and-impairments-user-profiles) and led an exercise to show the user journey of signing up flood warnings and the flow from a user research perspective. Some of the attendees were given instructions to use only their keyboard to complete the task, and others with their browser at 300% zoom to help simulate some of the access challenges that different users can face when trying to complete online services.
+ James has been compiling all the user research he can find about NRW web projects. He also set-up a survey on our dry weather blog to help understand what the page helps users to do 
+ Mary has continued running refresher training sessions for comms officers using Umbraco to produce content 
+ Our publishing team has continued accessibility checking content and publishing new consultations and guidance across our website, including updating our writing accessible documents guidance in line with current legislation 
+ Sophie met with a subject matter expert within the organisation to redesign our business finance intranet pages ahead of September. The new materials are currently with translation before we set them live next week 
+ Phil has been working with teams to troubleshoot online forms for our customers
