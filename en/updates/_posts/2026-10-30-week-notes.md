It's half term, so a few of the team are on leave. Here's what the rest have been up to:

## Species licensing: The flight continues 🐦 


+ Laura and Phil are continuing their work on the bird licensing project. They have begun working with the species team to review the current paper application forms, exploring why specific questions are asked, when users need to apply for a licence, and how they can better understand which licence they need. 

+ They are also drafting a blog about improvements to the change form process, including how six separate change forms were combined into a single service to make the process simpler and easier to use. 

+ The team has also continued to improve the change form based on user feedback, making it clearer, more efficient and easier for users to complete.
