## Here's what the Digital team has been up to this week

+ Laura and Sam mapped out the guidance, patterns and resources we have across the team and looked at who needs what, helping shape improvements to the content manual and team wiki as part of our work to strengthen standards and good digital practice. 

+ James has been arranging user testing to benchmark example tasks on the current intranet. He's been writing the discussion guide and setting up the interview tracker and note-taking templates. He also met with colleagues to discuss improvements to UX and accessibility in the Wales Environmental Information Portal.   

+ Mary has been working on a discovery of the 'About us' section of our website to tidy it up and streamline the content supported by Sophie. She's been looking at other exemplar websites to see how they do their 'About us' sections. 

+ Phil has been busy as a camp-maker prepping for Gov Camp Cymru on Friday. 

+ Laura and Sam met the Permissions Team to discuss changes to the service planned for next spring, and how this will impact the content and application form.  

+ The intranet team have been working on content strategy, standards and governance. They've been exploring SharePoint - documenting issues and constraints, and refining templates for different page types.  

+ Toyah has been doing a deep dive into dredging content with the marine licensing team. She also joined the digital transformation monthly showcase on Wednesday.  

+ Phil and Laura reviewed bird control applications, forms and guidance in more detail to understand what information applicants need to provide. They also looked at GOV.UK and NatureScot approaches and explored different bird licensing journeys. Based on the evidence gathered so far, they agreed to focus on a simple bird control journey, with improved information and support before users start an application.  

+ Laura analysed Welsh-language usage across four digital services, finding Welsh accounted for around 1.3% of submissions in 2025, this information will help inform the Use Your Welsh campaign.
