## Here's what our team has been up to this week:

+ We're excited to welcome a new content designer, Clare, to the Digital team and looking forward to meeting and working with her soon! 

+ On the intranet project, James has been running user testing interviews to benchmark tasks on the current intranet. These are providing invaluable insights into user behaviour and mental models, and reinforcing the importance of findability. Kim is currently exploring navigation and search in SharePoint. Sam and Jane have been drafting content and documenting SharePoint constraints. Alex and Sam are finalising the content governance framework and terms of reference for the Engagement and Advisory group. 

+ Sam and Laura have been mapping out the contents of the content manual and team wiki, and reviewing what each role/team needs. 

+ Laura and Phil have been designing the new 'apply for a bird control licence' form and meeting with the species team to do more fact finding. They've also published a [blog about the work they did on the 'change a licence' form](https://naturalresources.wales/footer-links/blog-nrw-digital/blog-post-making-it-easier-to-change-a-species-licence-online/?lang=en).  

+ Mary and Sophie have been continuing to work together on the discovery phase of the 'About us' section of our website. They've also been providing support on the DDAT bulletin and a programme for the DDAT day.  

+ Lucinda had a kick off meeting for the sign up/accounts project.  

+ Toyah and Lucinda started looking at adding more questions into the 'Check if you can use the land we manage' service and exploring possible flows in advance of their meeting with the subject matter expert next week.  

+ Last Friday several members of the team attended [GovCamp Cymru](https://www.govcamp.cymru), an 'unconference' on the theme of 'transforming public services'. The organisers and 'camp makers' did an amazing job, bringing together people from the public, private, 3rd sector and students to share and learn ideas on how we can deal with some of the common challenges we face. Heledd has written a [personal reflection on the day and themes from the sessions she attended](https://helivans.medium.com/govcamp-cymru-2026-46cd23c7b84d).
